Our Story

Handcrafted in the Heart of Da Nang

Since 2010, Lam Tuyen has been creating pure linen garments from a small studio inside Han Market. Every piece is cut and sewn by hand — made to fit your exact body, not a standard size chart.

We offer ten natural linen colours and tailor any garment to your measurements, whether you visit us in Da Nang or send your sizes from anywhere in the world.
