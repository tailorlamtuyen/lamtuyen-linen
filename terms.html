Measurement Responsibility

For bespoke garments, accurate measurements are your responsibility. We provide a Measurement Guide to assist you. If measurements are provided inaccurately, we are not liable for poor fit. We recommend having measurements taken by a professional tailor in your area.

Non-Refundable Once Production Begins

Custom and bespoke orders are non-refundable once production has commenced. Production begins only after you have confirmed the quote and payment is received. We will notify you before starting production.

Before production begins, you may cancel your order for a full refund of any deposit paid. Once production has started, the full order amount is due even if the order is cancelled.

Minor Adjustments

If you are able to visit our workshop in Da Nang, we offer one free fitting and minor adjustment session for bespoke garments. For international orders, minor alterations can be done locally by a tailor of your choosing — we recommend budgeting for this as bespoke garments sometimes require minor local adjustment.

5. Ready-to-Wear Orders

Returns — Ready-to-Wear Only

We accept returns of ready-to-wear garments under the following conditions:

  • The item is unworn, unwashed, and in its original condition with all tags attached
  • Return is requested within 14 days of delivery (confirmed by tracking)
  • The item is not a sale or discounted item
  • Return shipping cost is paid by the customer
  • Items must be returned by tracked shipping service

Once we receive and inspect the returned item, we will issue a refund of the garment price (excluding original shipping cost) within 5–10 business days to the original payment method.

No Returns on Custom Items

As stated above, custom and bespoke garments made to your measurements cannot be returned or refunded once production has begun.
