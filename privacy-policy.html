1. Who We Are

Lam Tuyen Linen ("we", "us", "our") is a linen clothing atelier based at Han Market, Da Nang, Vietnam, trading since 2010. We sell ready-to-wear linen garments and offer bespoke custom tailoring services, shipping worldwide. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use our website at lamtuyenlinen.com or submit an order request.

2. What Data We Collect

Information You Provide Directly

  • Contact details: Full name, email address, phone number (with country dial code)
  • Shipping address: Street address, city, postal code, country
  • Order details: Garment type, colour preferences, size (ready-to-wear orders), style notes and special requests
  • Body measurements (bespoke / custom orders only): Chest/bust, waist, hips, shoulder width, sleeve length, back width, torso length, height, inseam, and desired garment length — all in centimetres
  • Payment information: We do not collect or store any payment card data. Payment is handled separately via bank transfer, cash, or agreed payment methods after your quote is confirmed.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Cookie preferences: Saved in your browser's localStorage to remember your consent choices
  • Analytics data (only if you consent): Page views, session duration, referral source — collected via privacy-respecting analytics. No data is collected if you select "Essential Only".

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data for the following purposes:

  • Order processing: To review your order request, prepare a price quote, confirm production details, and arrange shipping
  • Communication: To contact you within 24 hours with your quote, and to follow up on order status
  • Shipping coordination: To ship your completed garments to your address
  • Record-keeping: To maintain accurate order records for our business operations
  • Legal compliance: To comply with applicable tax, trade, and legal obligations

We do not use your data for unsolicited marketing, sell it to third parties, or use it for automated decision-making or profiling.

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

For visitors from the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we process your data under the following lawful bases:

  • Contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): Processing your name, contact details, address, and order details is necessary to fulfil your order request and any resulting contract
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): Keeping business records and responding to enquiries
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): Analytics cookies, if you choose "Accept All" in our cookie banner

5. Data Processors & Third Parties

We share your data only with trusted service providers acting as data processors on our behalf:

  • Supabase (supabase.com): Secure cloud database where your order information is stored. Supabase is SOC 2 Type II compliant and stores data in secure data centres. Supabase Privacy Policy →
  • Resend (resend.com): Transactional email service used to send us notification of your order. Your email address is included in this notification. Resend Privacy Policy →
  • Vercel (vercel.com): Website hosting provider. May log IP addresses for security and performance purposes. Vercel Privacy Policy →
  • Google Fonts: We load fonts from Google's servers. Google may receive your IP address when fonts are loaded. Google Privacy Policy →

All processors are contractually bound to use your data only as instructed by us and to maintain appropriate security measures.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected:

  • Order records (including measurements): 2 years from the date of your order request, to allow for repeat orders, alterations, and to comply with any applicable legal obligations
  • Cookie consent records: Stored in your browser's localStorage until you clear it or change your preferences
  • Email communications: Retained for 2 years in our email inbox

After the retention period, data is deleted or anonymised.

7. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your home country, including the United States (where Supabase and Resend infrastructure is located). Where required by law (e.g., GDPR), such transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards including standard contractual clauses or equivalent mechanisms.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Deletion ("right to be forgotten"): Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Restriction: Request that we restrict processing of your data in certain circumstances
  • Portability (EEA/UK): Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Objection (EEA/UK): Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdraw consent: Where processing is based on consent (e.g., analytics cookies), withdraw consent at any time without affecting prior processing
  • Non-discrimination (California/CCPA): We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at tailorlamtuyen@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Cookies & Local Storage

Our website uses:

  • Essential localStorage entries: Cookie consent preferences (required for the site to remember your choices). These do not require consent.
  • Analytics cookies (optional, consent-based): If you click "Accept All" on our cookie banner, we may collect anonymised analytics data to understand how visitors use our site. No personal identifiers are included. You can change your preference at any time by clearing your browser's localStorage or clicking "Manage Preferences" in the cookie banner.

We do not use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking, or fingerprinting.

10. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), access-controlled databases, and trusted infrastructure providers. However, no transmission over the internet is 100% secure. If you have concerns about the security of your data, please contact us.

11. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed to children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a child, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.

12. Jurisdiction-Specific Rights

European Economic Area & United Kingdom (GDPR / UK GDPR)

You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. A list of EU supervisory authorities is available at edpb.europa.eu. UK residents may contact the ICO at ico.org.uk.

California (CCPA / CPRA)

California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected, the right to delete, the right to opt-out of sale (we do not sell data), and the right to non-discrimination. Submit requests to tailorlamtuyen@gmail.com.

Canada (PIPEDA)

Canadian residents may contact us to access or correct personal information. You may also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at priv.gc.ca.

Japan (APPI)

Japanese residents may request disclosure, correction, or deletion of their personal information. Contact us at tailorlamtuyen@gmail.com.

Vietnam (PDPD)

We comply with Vietnam's Personal Data Protection Decree. Vietnamese residents have the right to access, correct, delete, and object to processing of their personal data.
